Improvements on Recycling

Edmund Rice College (ERC)

Based on the data and infomation, Edmund Rice College manage recycling programs through a hands-on approach binnual waste audits like the ECO-ERC student team that collects, weighs, and organize the waster into different categories. THis is because Students volunteer to learn and help with recycling by analyzing waste data to improve recycling, reduce contamination, and increase the usage of recycling bins across the school.

Trinity Catholic College (Adamstown)

The Trinity Catholic College handles the situation by collecting and transforming the waste into different categories by students who are interested, making them into products such as 'robots', 'metal', 'wood', and 'jewellery'. For classrooms, they specifically add bins for paper and carboard, with selected classrooms ultilising compost bins for food scrap materials. In addition with the help of a Fantastic Plastic Club, they focus on turning other plastic waste into new, sustainable products.

St Mary's Catholic College (Gateshead)

The St Mary's Catholic College manages recycling through a student-led stewardship program, focusing on waste reduction and recycling. Major projects inluce a 'Return and Earn' program, composting and the use of sustainable, reusable resources in their cafe to minimise landfill waste. Behind the scenes, exercising stweardship means caring for the gifts God has given us, including the environment, talents, and resources. Students are encouraged to take responsibility for their actions and decision on making a positive impact on the world around them and their school community.

St Francis Xavier's College (Edmondson Park)

St Francis Xavier's College faces the same challenges seen across NSW, they manage their recycling through a student-led environmental club (SEALS) and participating in the 'Return and Earn' program. They use a house-base competition to not just to organise waste successfully but also to encourage students to into collecting bottles and cans for donation to charity.
