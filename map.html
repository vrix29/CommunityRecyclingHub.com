What Can I Drop Off?

  • Paper & cardboard
  • Glass bottles & jars
  • Plastic containers (1–7)
  • Steel & aluminium cans
  • Green waste & compost
  • Electronics (Devices)
  • E-waste (phones, TVs, computers)

Tips Before You Go

  • Rinse containers before dropping off
  • Keep lids on bottles & jars
  • Flatten cardboard boxes
  • Bag loose soft plastics separately
  • Check opening hours before visiting

About Hunter Region Recycling

Resource Recovery Centres

Major facilities operated by councils such as Newcastle City Council, Lake Macquarie City Council, and Maitland City Council. These centres accept a wide range of household waste and separate materials for reuse or recycling.

Kerbside Recycling

Most homes in the Hunter Region have a yellow-lid bin for recycling collected fortnightly. Materials are sorted at a Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) and sent to manufacturers to be made into new products.

E-Waste Drop-Off

Since July 2022, e-waste is banned from NSW landfill. Drop off old electronics at dedicated e-waste points for free. Items include TVs, computers, phones, printers, and small appliances.

Green Waste Composting

Garden and food scraps collected through the lime-green lid bin or dropped at a resource recovery centre are processed into compost — keeping organic matter out of landfill and improving local soils.
