Helping Our Community Reduce Waste

Every year the Hunter Region generates millions of tonnes of waste — yet roughly one in three kilograms that ends up in general rubbish could have been recycled or composted instead.

This website explores recycling habits in our local community and shows how small, everyday actions can protect the environment. Use the interactive map to find your nearest drop-off station, explore the data, and learn what you can do to make a difference.

Why Recycling Matters
