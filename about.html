About This Project

A student-led initiative from St Francis Xavier's College exploring recycling habits in the Hunter Region and encouraging better waste practices in our community.

Our Mission

This website was created as part of a Year 11 Science project at St Francis Xavier's College (SFX) in the Hunter Region, NSW. Our goal is to raise awareness about the recycling challenges facing our local community and provide practical tools — like providing a interactive map and real data — that make it easier for people to understand how to recycle correctly.

We believe that our actions add up. When individuals understand what goes in which bin, where to drop off e-waste, and how much of our rubbish actually gets recycled, they make better decisions — and that benefits the entire Hunter Region.

The Problem We're Addressing

Contamination

When the wrong items go into the recycling bin, entire loads can be rejected and sent to landfill. Nationally, the average contamination rate in yellow-lid bins is 14% — a figure that education can directly reduce.

Lack of Awareness

Many students and community members are unsure what can and cannot be recycled. This uncertainty leads to either contamination (putting wrong items in) or wish-cycling — throwing items in and hoping for the best.

Hard to Find Drop-Off Points

Items like e-waste, household chemicals, and soft plastics require special drop-off locations. Without a clear, accessible guide to where these are, people default to general rubbish — sending recyclable material to landfill.

What We Want to Achieve

01

Educate the Community

Teach students and the community about proper recycling practices and the importance of reducing waste. It gives a clear understanding of how to recycle correctly and the impact of contamination on the environment.

02

Make Recycling Easier to Access

Provide an interactive map of all recycling stations across Australia such as the 'Hunter Region' so residents can quickly find where to drop off any type of waste.

03

Inspire Better Habits at School

Highlight the recycling challenges at EMC, Trinity Catholic College, and St Mary, SFX for practical solutions — better bin labelling, food scrap systems, and in-class education — that other schools can get involved too.
